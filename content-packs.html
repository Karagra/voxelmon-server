Content packs

Changes to a server's world made of data alone: no code. A pack adds to the game's loot and to where its Voxelmon appear, using the game's own items and species.

Built: loot and spawns. New items, blocks, models, recipes and shop goods are not yet: each needs players' headsets to be told of them, and comes with a later game update.

What a pack is

A pack is a folder in the server's packs/ folder. It holds a pack.toml and its rule files:

packs/
  approved.toml              # written by `pack approve`
  more-iron/
    pack.toml
    loot/dungeons.toml
    spawns/snow.toml
# pack.toml
id = "more-iron"             # letters, digits, - and _
name = "More iron"
version = "1.0.0"

Packs run nothing: the server reads them and checks every name. Even so, a pack is used only once the owner approves it, as plugins are:

> packs
  more-iron 1.0.0 (More iron)  waiting: `pack approve ID`
> pack approve more-iron
Pack more-iron approved and in use.

The approval is for the pack's files as they are: change any file and the pack waits to be approved again. A pack may hold 4 MB at most.

Loot

Each table in a loot/*.toml file is one rule: whenever loot of its kind is rolled, there is chance percent that it also holds the item, least to most of it.

# loot/dungeons.toml
[more_iron]
source = "dungeon_chest"
item = "IRON INGOT"
least = 1
most = 3
chance = 25
KeyWhat it is
sourcechest (towns and the wild), dungeon_chest, supply_cache, creature (a beaten Voxelmon), battle (a won battle), fishing or other
itemAn item's name as the game shows it, in any case
least, mostHow many, 1 up to the item's stack limit
chancePercent, 1 to 100

Spawns

Each table in a spawns/*.toml file is one rule: of the wild Voxelmon appearing in its biomes at its time of day, chance percent are its species instead, at a level from least_level to most_level. The first rule that matches and wins its roll is taken.

# spawns/snow.toml
[lunapups_at_night]
species = "LUNAPUP"
biomes = ["SNOWY TAIGA", "SNOW"]
least_level = 5
most_level = 12
time = "night"
chance = 10
KeyWhat it is
speciesA species' name, in any case
biomesBiome names as a plugin's World.biome gives them; leave it out for anywhere
least_level, most_levelIts level, 1 to 100
timeday, night or any
chancePercent of the matching spawns, 1 to 100

A pack's spawns are wild spawns only: dungeons, dens, hives, fishing and guardians keep their own. A wild Voxelmon of any species can appear where a rule sends it, swimmers too, so keep sea species to sea biomes.

With plugins

Packs are applied first, then plugins hear of it: a plugin's LootRolled sees a pack's items among the loot, and its CreatureSpawning sees the pack's species, and either may change them again.

Not yet

New items, blocks, furniture and their models; recipes; shop goods; new Voxelmon species, sounds and music. A recipe or a shop's goods must be known to every player's headset as well as the server, and a model or texture must be downloaded to it; each comes with a later game update, and this page will say when.
