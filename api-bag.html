Bag

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From player.bag().

What a player carries: the backpack's slots, its side slots, the strap pockets, the belt's cubes and both hands.

Description

A player's things are where they put them, as in the game: the backpack has 16 slots inside, holsters on its sides, pockets on its straps, and the belt holds 4 capture cubes. Reading needs nothing; changing needs player_data. A change shows on the player's own pack at once.
