Town

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From world.town_at(...), world.towns_near(...) and player.town.

A town of the world: its place, its people's clinic and shops, and the Voxelord threatening it.

Properties

TypeNameDescription
StringnameIts name.
PlacecentreIts plaza's middle.
BlockPosfromOne corner of its land.
BlockPostoThe other corner.
boolfreedWhether its Voxelord has been beaten.
SpeciesvoxelordThe species of its Voxelord.
PlacelairWhere its Voxelord waits.
Place?clinicIts VoxelAid clinic, where Voxelmon are healed.

Related events: PlayerEnteredTown, TownFreed.
