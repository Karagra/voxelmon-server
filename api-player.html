Player

Available. Every call here works on servers today.

Handle: stands for something in the game; its calls fail with Gone once it is gone.

Description

A Player is someone in the world now. Their lasting id stays the same every time they join (it comes from their store account through Epic): keep that, not the Player, to remember someone. Once they leave, the Player is gone and its calls fail; what is kept of them is a PlayerProfile.

Reading needs nothing. Changing a player's bag, gear, coins, progress or team needs player_data.

PlayerProfile

Plain data, copied: fields in both directions.

A player who has played here, whether in the world now or not.

FieldDescription
id: PlayerIdString id()Their lasting ID.
name: StringString name()Their name when last seen.
first_joined: Timelong firstJoined()When they first joined.
last_seen: Timelong lastSeen()When they last left (now, if in the world).
blocked: boolboolean blocked()Whether they are on the blocked list.
online: boolboolean online()Whether they are in the world now.
