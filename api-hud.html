Hud

Available. Every call here works on servers today.

Handle: stands for something in the game; its calls fail with Gone once it is gone.

A small scoreboard by the player's watch: a title and up to 12 lines, each with a value at its right and a bar under it; with boss bars and server tabs on the watch.

MethodDescription
fn create(title: &str) -> Result<Hud>static Hud create(String title)An empty HUD (a title of at most 24 letters).
fn set_title(&self, title: &str) -> Result<()>void setTitle(String title)Changes its title.
fn set_line(&self, index: i64, text: &str, value: &str, colour: Colour) -> Result<()>void setLine(long index, String text, String value, Colour colour)Sets line index (0 to 11): its text, the value at its right (may be empty) and their colour. A bar it has stays.
fn set_bar(&self, index: i64, fraction: f64, colour: Colour) -> Result<()>void setBar(long index, double fraction, Colour colour)Gives line index a bar under its text, fraction (0 to 1) full.
fn clear_line(&self, index: i64) -> Result<()>void clearLine(long index)Empties line index, its bar too.
fn show(&self, player: Player) -> Result<()>void show(Player player)Shows it to a player, in place of any HUD they had.
fn hide(&self, player: Player) -> Result<()>void hide(Player player)Takes it away from a player.
fn remove(&self) -> Result<()>void remove()Takes it away from everyone and forgets it.

Description

A server has three places of its own in each player's view, all drawn by their game from what the plugin says, so they cost the server nothing a frame and are sent only when they change.

A HUD stands just below the player's watch face, where they glance as at a watch, rather than fixed in their eyes (which is tiring in a headset): a title and up to 12 lines, numbered 0 to 11. Each line is text (at most 24 letters) with a value at its right (at most 12) in one colour, and may have a bar under it (set_bar, its fraction 0 to 1). A player is shown one HUD at a time: showing them another takes the first away.

A BossBar hangs above the player's line of sight, in front of them, turning with them a moment behind: a title over a bar, for a boss's health or an event's countdown. A player is shown at most two.

A server tab (Player set_wrist_tab) puts a Menu's first tab on the player's watch beside its own pages, at most two a player: its first 24 elements, pressed as on the menu (the same MenuPressed, with tab 0). Changes to the menu show there at the end of the tick. Neither shows in a demo or when playing alone.

Letters outside plain ASCII show as ?.

BossBar

Handle: stands for something in the game; its calls fail with Gone once it is gone.

A bar above the player's view: a title over a bar, at most two a player.
