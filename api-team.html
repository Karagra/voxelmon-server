Team

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From player.team().

A player's Voxelmon: the four cubes on their belt, and those stored.

Description

The belt holds the cubes a player carries into battle; the rest are stored and reached at a clinic or on the watch. Adding and removing needs creatures.

Properties

TypeNameDescription
List[Creature?]beltThe four belt places, in order.
List[Creature]storedEvery stored Voxelmon.
intstored_limitHow many may be stored.

Methods

ReturnsMethodDescription
List[Creature]all()Belt and stored together.
booladd(creature: Creature) creaturesAdds one: to the belt if a place is free, else stored; false if full.
nothingremove(creature: Creature) creaturesTakes one away from the player for good.
nothingheal_all()Heals every one, as at a clinic.
nothingmove_to_belt(creature: Creature, place: int) creaturesPuts a stored one on the belt, swapping out what was there.
