Dungeon

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From world.dungeon_at(...), world.dungeons_near(...) and player.dungeon.

A dungeon of the world: its tier, its levels, its guardian and its reset.

Properties

TypeNameDescription
StringnameIts name, as its banner shows.
PlaceentranceWhere its stairs begin.
DungeonTiertierCopper, iron, cobalt or voxelium.
intlowest_levelIts creatures' lowest level.
inthighest_levelIts creatures' highest level (its guardian's).
boolcorruptedWhether it is corrupted this time (stronger, better loot).
boolbeatenWhether its guardian is beaten and it waits to reset.
intresetsHow many times it has reset.

Methods

ReturnsMethodDescription
nothingreset() world_editResets it now: chests refilled, guardian back (no player may be inside).

Events

Related events: PlayerEnteredDungeon, DungeonBeaten, DungeonReset.
