Console commands

Type these into the server's window (or its terminal). Type help to list them.

CommandWhat it does
statusThe world, the players in it, when it was last saved, and how long the server's ticks take.
codeThis server's join code.
listThe players in the world, each with a number. Commands below take that number.
kick NPuts player N out. They may join again.
block NPuts player N out and never lets them in again, kept in blocked.txt.
blockedThe blocked list, numbered.
unblock NTakes entry N off the blocked list.
saveSaves the world now.
stopTells players, saves and stops the server. Ctrl-C does the same.

Players and their numbers

list shows each player with their store account's name, numbered from 1:

list
  1  KARAGRA (store account)
  2  NERY (store account)
  3  SKIP#0377 (store account), no pose arriving

"No pose arriving" means that player's headset is sending no movement (it is asleep or has lost tracking); they stand still to everyone until it comes back.

A number after a name (#0377) appears only while two players have the same name, so you can tell them apart. A number belongs to a player while they are in the world; check list again before kick or block if players have come and gone.

Blocking needs Epic to have confirmed who the player is, which happens within a few seconds of them joining. A blocked player stays blocked across restarts until you unblock them.
