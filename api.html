API reference

Being built. 289 of 295 calls and 19 of 55 events work on servers today; every page marks the rest not yet.

Every type a plugin uses, the same in Rust and Java: pick the language at the top of the page and every signature follows. The Events page lists everything a plugin can react to.

In Rust a call returns voxelmon::Result<T>; in Java it throws ApiException, and a value that may be missing is an Optional. A need beside a method names what the plugin's manifest must ask for (needs); everything else only reads.

PageWhat it is
EventsEverything a plugin can react to. Some events come before something happens and can stop it; the rest say it has happened.
ServerThe running server: its players, world, settings, console and this plugin's own settings and log.
PluginWhat a plugin is: the code the server calls as it starts, as the world becomes ready and as it stops.
SchedulerRunning code later, or again and again.
PermissionsWho may do what: operators and permission names.
StorageData a plugin keeps between runs, for itself and for each player, its own files, and the internet.
PlayerA player in the world: their body, health, bag, gear, coins, progress and team.
BagWhat a player carries: the backpack's 16 slots, its side slots, the strap pockets, the belt's 4 cubes and both hands.
ItemStackSome of one item: what it is and how many.
EquipmentWhat a player wears and holds ready: the ten slots of the page inside their backpack's door.
ProfessionsA player's twelve professions: experience and levels.
MessagesTelling players things: notices by the watch, large banners, floating labels in the world and sounds.
MenuA panel a plugin shows: tabs down its left side, and in each tab text, buttons, item tiles, toggles, sliders and Voxelmon.
WorldThe world: blocks, placed objects, loose items, Voxelmon, time, weather, towns and dungeons.
BlockOne block in the world: what it is and where.
PlacedObjectSomething placed that is more than a block: furniture, stations, chests, beds, doors, crops.
RegionA named box of the world a plugin watches: players entering and leaving it are told to the plugin.
TownA town: its place, its clinic, and the Voxelord threatening it.
DungeonA dungeon: its tier, its levels, its guardian and its reset.
CreatureOne Voxelmon: wild, a player's companion, or kept in a player's team.
SpeciesA kind of Voxelmon: its essences, its moves, where it lives and what it becomes.
TeamA player's Voxelmon: the four cubes on their belt, and those stored.
BattleA turn-based battle between a player's Voxelmon and a wild one, a trainer's or a guardian.
TypesThe small values every page uses: places, block positions, IDs, colours, times and settings.
