Description

Regions are how a plugin makes spawn protection, claims, arenas or shops: it listens for players entering and leaving, and stops breaking and placing inside. A region is the plugin's own and is kept with its data; it costs one check when a player's feet change block.

Related events: PlayerEnteredRegion, PlayerLeftRegion.
