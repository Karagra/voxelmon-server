Equipment

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From player.equipment().

What a player wears and holds ready: the ten slots of the equipment page inside their backpack's door.

Description

Gear goes on and comes off only through these slots, as on the page in the game. The two hand slots are the weapon and shield holstered at the shoulders, drawn by reaching over them.

Properties

TypeNameDescription
intdefenseDefense from everything worn.
intattackAttack of the main-hand weapon.
intblockBlock of the off-hand shield.

Methods

ReturnsMethodDescription
ItemStack?get(slot: GearSlot)What a slot holds.
nothingset(slot: GearSlot, stack: ItemStack?) player_dataPuts a piece on (it must suit the slot), or takes it off to nowhere.
nothingswap_hands() player_dataSwaps the main-hand and off-hand pieces, as SWAP does.
