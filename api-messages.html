Messages

Being built. 4 of 10 calls here work on servers today; the rest are marked not yet and answer that they are not available. Names may still change until the first plugin release.

Reached from the server: server.messages().

Telling players things: notices by the watch, large banners, floating labels in the world and sounds.

MethodDescription
fn notice(&self, player: Player, text: &str) -> Result<()>void notice(Player player, String text)A short notice by their watch, with a soft sound (at most 80 letters).
fn notice_all(&self, text: &str) -> Result<()>void noticeAll(String text)A notice to every player.
fn banner(&self, player: Player, banner: Banner) -> Result<()>void banner(Player player, Banner banner)A banner before them.
fn banner_all(&self, banner: Banner) -> Result<()>void bannerAll(Banner banner)A banner before every player.
fn label(&self, place: Place, lines: Vec<String>) -> Result<Label>Label label(Place place, List<String> lines) not yetText floating in the world, turned to each viewer (at most 4 lines of 32 letters).
fn play_sound(&self, place: Place, sound: Sound) -> Result<()>void playSound(Place place, Sound sound) not yetA sound heard by players near it, from there.

Description

There is no text chat in VoxelMon, so plugins speak through these. Text may use any letters, numbers and common signs of English; others show as ?. A notice is short and fades; a banner is large and brief, as a dungeon's name is when entered; a label stays where it is put.

Label

Handle: stands for something in the game; its calls fail with Gone once it is gone.

Floating text a plugin placed.

MethodDescription
fn set_lines(&self, lines: Vec<String>) -> Result<()>void setLines(List<String> lines) not yetChanges its text.
fn move_to(&self, place: Place) -> Result<()>void moveTo(Place place) not yetMoves it.
fn set_reach(&self, blocks: f64) -> Result<()>void setReach(double blocks) not yetHow far off it can be read (8 to 48; it fades beyond).
fn remove(&self) -> Result<()>void remove() not yetTakes it away.

Banner

Plain data, copied: fields in both directions.

A large title and a smaller line, shown for a few seconds.

FieldDescription
title: StringString title()The large line (at most 32 letters).
subtitle: StringString subtitle()The smaller line under it (at most 48 letters).
colour: ColourColour colour()The title's colour.
seconds: f64double seconds()How long it shows, 1 to 10.

Sound

Plain data, copied: fields in both directions.

One of the game's sounds, by name, with a volume and a pitch.

FieldDescription
name: StringString name()The sound's name; the list is in the server's sounds command.
volume: f64double volume()0 to 1.
pitch: f64double pitch()0.5 to 2; 1 as recorded.
