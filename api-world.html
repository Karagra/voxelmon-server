World

Available. Every call here works on servers today.

Reached from the server: server.world().

The world: blocks, placed objects, loose items, Voxelmon, time, weather, towns and dungeons.

fn time_of_day(&self) -> Result<f64>double timeOfDay()Where the day is, 0 to 1: 0 dawn, 0.4 dusk (20 minutes of day, 10 of night).
fn is_night(&self) -> Result<bool>boolean isNight()Whether it is night.
fn weather(&self) -> Result<Weather>Weather weather()The weather: what falls and how hard (a spell's own strength while it rains, not its fading), or a plugin's held weather.

False for an unknown trade or town, the clinic's healer (who sells nothing), or goods past the limits.

Description

The world is loaded around the players: a block far from everyone is not loaded, and reading it gives nothing. Changing blocks, objects or loose items needs world_edit; spawning Voxelmon needs creatures.

LooseItem

Handle: stands for something in the game; its calls fail with Gone once it is gone.

Weather

Plain data, copied: fields in both directions.

The weather.

FieldDescription
kind: WeatherKindWeatherKind kind()What falls.
strength: f64double strength()How strong, 0 to 1.

WeatherKind

One of a fixed set of values.

What falls from the sky.

ValueMeaning
WeatherKind::ClearWeatherKind.CLEARNothing.
WeatherKind::RainWeatherKind.RAINRain (snow where it is cold).
WeatherKind::StormWeatherKind.STORMA thunderstorm (a snowstorm where it is cold).
