Player

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From server.players(), events, or server.player(id).

A player in the world: their body, health, bag, gear, coins, progress and team.

Description

A Player is someone in the world now. Their lasting id stays the same every time they join (it comes from their store account through Epic); keep that, not the Player, to remember someone. A player who has left is a PlayerProfile, readable and, for saved values, changeable until they return.

Reading needs nothing. Changing a player's bag, gear, coins, progress or team needs player_data; moving or healing them needs nothing more than the plugin being approved.
