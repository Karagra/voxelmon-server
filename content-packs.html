Content packs

Changes to a server's world made of data alone: no code. A pack adds to the game's loot and to where its Voxelmon appear, brings its own models (furniture, props and statues every player sees, streamed to their headsets as they come near), its own Voxelmon species and battle moves, its own items, the recipes that make them at the game's stations, and goods for the towns' shops.

Built: loot, spawns, models, objects, species, moves, items, recipes and shop goods. Not yet: sounds and music, item behaviours of a pack's own, code run on headsets, world generation (see the end of this page).

What a pack is

A pack is a folder in the server's packs/ folder. It holds a pack.toml and its rule files:

packs/
  approved.toml              # written by `pack approve`
  more-iron/
    pack.toml
    loot/dungeons.toml
    spawns/snow.toml
    models/throne.bbmodel
    objects/furniture.toml
    species/glimmer.toml
    moves/glimmer.toml
    items/gems.toml
    recipes/gems.toml
    shops/gems.toml
# pack.toml
id = "more-iron"             # letters, digits, - and _
name = "More iron"
version = "1.0.0"

Packs run nothing: the server reads them and checks every name. Even so, a pack is used only once the owner approves it, as plugins are:

> packs
  more-iron 1.0.0 (More iron)  waiting: `pack approve ID`
> pack approve more-iron
Pack more-iron approved and in use.

The approval is for the pack's files as they are: change any file and the pack waits to be approved again. A pack may hold 4 MB at most.

Loot

Each table in a loot/*.toml file is one rule: whenever loot of its kind is rolled, there is chance percent that it also holds the item, least to most of it.

# loot/dungeons.toml
[more_iron]
source = "dungeon_chest"
item = "IRON INGOT"
least = 1
most = 3
chance = 25
KeyWhat it is
sourcechest (towns and the wild), dungeon_chest, supply_cache, creature (a beaten Voxelmon), battle (a won battle), fishing or other
itemAn item's name as the game shows it, in any case
least, mostHow many, 1 up to the item's stack limit
chancePercent, 1 to 100

Spawns

Each table in a spawns/*.toml file is one rule: of the wild Voxelmon appearing in its biomes at its time of day, chance percent are its species instead, at a level from least_level to most_level. The first rule that matches and wins its roll is taken.

# spawns/snow.toml
[lunapups_at_night]
species = "LUNAPUP"
biomes = ["SNOWY TAIGA", "SNOW"]
least_level = 5
most_level = 12
time = "night"
chance = 10
KeyWhat it is
speciesA species' name, in any case, or a pack's species by its key (pack:table)
biomesBiome names as a plugin's World.biome gives them; leave it out for anywhere
least_level, most_levelIts level, 1 to 100
timeday, night or any
chancePercent of the matching spawns, 1 to 100

A pack's spawns are wild spawns only: dungeons, dens, hives, fishing and guardians keep their own. A wild Voxelmon of any species can appear where a rule sends it, swimmers too, so keep sea species to sea biomes.

Models

Every Blockbench file in a pack's models/ folder is a model, named pack:file (more-iron:throne for models/throne.bbmodel): cubes painted on the file's own textures, its groups as bones and its animations as clips (idle, walk, run, sit, attack and three of its own). The server converts them as it starts and sends each to a player's headset the first time something made from it comes near them, checked and kept by its SHA-256, so nothing is installed. A model is at most 512 cubes, 32 groups and 8 clips.

A group or cube named with a # marks a place for the game and is not drawn:

NameWhat it marks
#seatWhere a player sits: a cube's top face's middle, or a group's pivot
#collisionA box players and creatures meet (up to 8: #collision_back, #collision2...); with none, the whole model is one box
#gripWhere a hand closes on a held item (a tool, a weapon, a bow, a shield): the middle of the cube, the haft running along its longest side toward the #head (or toward the far end of the model), so a model authored lying along X or Z is held as one standing up its Y. A group's pivot and its own up (+Y) mark it too. A bow's must run up the model's Y (its string does)
#headA tool's or weapon's working end (an axe's head, a sword's blade): what a swing strikes with, as the game's own tools strike with theirs. With none, the half of the item past the middle of its grip along the haft
#stringA bow's string, the line the arrow is drawn back along

Held, lying on the ground, placed, or a Voxelmon pointed at, a model is outlined as the game's own are: along its own edges, in its own colours, found on the headset from its cubes and its texture's clear texels as it arrives.

Objects

An object is something placed in the world made from a model: furniture, a prop, a statue, a body. It stands on the cells its model fills (its size rounded up to whole blocks, at most 16 a side), turned to a quarter turn, its model's front (Blockbench's north, -Z) toward where it faces. Players meet its collision boxes, sit on its seat, open it if it stores, and break it if it may be broken (once emptied, as a chest); a broken one is gone, as no item drops for it. Objects are saved with the world by their model's name: if a pack is taken away, its objects stay, as small grey cubes nobody can break, and come back as they were when it returns.

Each table in an objects/*.toml file says how objects of one model are made. A model with no table can still be placed, with the defaults.

# objects/furniture.toml
[throne]
model = "throne"           # a model of this pack, or pack:file of another
name = "OAK THRONE"
seat = true
storage = 0
breakable = true
light = 0
KeyWhat it is
modelIts model: a file of this pack's models/ without .bbmodel, or another pack's as pack:file
nameWhat it is called, at most 32 letters (optional)
seattrue: players sit at its #seat (which it must have); false: never. Left out: where it marks one
storageSlots it stores in, 0 to 24 (default 0). A click opens it; its model's open clip plays while it stands open
breakableWhether players may break it (default true)
lightThe light it gives, 0 to 15 (default 0; a torch is 14)

Players place objects from items a pack makes for them (an item of behaviour object, made at a station by its recipe); broken, such an object drops its item again. Plugins place them too (World.place_model, PlacedObject.play for an animation, Model) and so may the owner, at the console:

> place more-iron:throne 12 64 -3 90
Placed more-iron:throne (object 41).

An example pack (a chair with a seat, a lamp that lights, a chest of twelve slots whose lid opens; the chair also a CAMP CHAIR item struck at the workbench) is in the game's source as plugins-examples/packs/furniture; the example plugin Spawn Guard places its chair and lamp beside spawn.

Species

Each table in a species/*.toml file is a Voxelmon species of the server's own, keyed pack:table (creatures:glimmerkit). Players catch it, train it, battle with it and see it on the watch and in battles as any other: the server sends every player's headset the species (and its model, as it is first needed) as they join, so nothing is installed. Each player keeps the Voxelmon they own in their file on the server, by the species' key: if its pack is taken away, they keep it (stored, as a plain placeholder nobody can send out) and it is itself again when the pack returns.

# species/glimmer.toml
[glimmerkit]
name = "GLIMMERKIT"
model = "glimmerkit"
span = 0.7
health = 9
walk_speed = 0.4
essence = "lightning"
attack = 8
defense = 7
speed = 9
rarity = "uncommon"
capture_difficulty = 40
hours = "day"
temperament = "friendly"
night_temperament = "neutral"
habitats = ["grass", "savanna"]
moves = ["1 TACKLE", "1 spark_nip", "6 static_veil", "12 BITE"]
loot = ["STRING"]
cry = "LUNAPUP"
cry_pitch = 130
KeyWhat it is
nameIts name, the game's way: capitals, at most 16 letters, digits, spaces, -, ' and .; never one of the game's own species' names. Make it your own: names of another game's creatures are not allowed on the stores
modelIts model (a file of this pack's models/, or pack:file): animated, with at least an idle and a walk clip; run, sit and attack are shown where it has them. Its front faces Blockbench's north (-Z)
spanIts longest side, 0.05 to 6 (the game's own are 0.4 to 1.5); the model's proportions give the rest. Default 0.6
healthIts base health, 1 to 200 (the game's own are 6 to 16). Default 10
walk_speed, hoverWalking pace and how high it floats, 0 to 4 (defaults 0.35 and 0)
essence, second_essenceOne of air, water, fire, earth, dragon, frost, insect, lightning, metal, shadow, plant, poison; the second optional
attack, defense, speedBase battle stats, 1 to 100 (the game's own are 4 to 14). Default 5
raritycommon, uncommon or rare (default common): how often it is rolled against the others where it lives
capture_difficulty0 to 100, higher harder (default 40)
hoursalways, day or night (default always)
temperament, night_temperamentfriendly, neutral (attacks when riled) or aggressive (hunts players); by night the day's if left out
locomotionwalk, swim or amphibious (default walk); pounce and hop true or false
habitatsWhere it spawns by itself: grass, savanna, mud, sand, snow, autumn, taiga, jungle, coast, rock, fungal, volcanic, moor, cave, lush_cave, flooded_cave, volcanic_cave, ice_cave, crystal_cave, dungeon, shallows, reef, open_sea, lake. None: only where a spawn rule or a plugin puts it
movesUp to 24, each "LEVEL MOVE" in level order: a move of this pack by its table, another pack's as pack:table, or one of the game's by its name. One or two at level 1
evolves_into, evolves_atThe species it becomes (of this pack by its table, pack:table, or one of the game's by name) and at what level, 2 to 100
lootUp to two items it drops when beaten: the game's by name, a pack's by key (gems:ruby) or this pack's by its table; none for its essence's own. A pack's item it names must be there (approve its pack too), else the species is refused
cry, cry_pitchThe game's species whose cry it calls with (default: the first of its essence) and that cry's pitch, 50 to 200 percent. No sound files travel

Its picture on the watch and in battles is drawn on each headset from its model, from the front a little from above, in its idle pose. A server has at most 256 species of its own.

Moves

Each table in a moves/*.toml file is a battle move of the server's own, keyed pack:table. Battles are worked out on the server; players' headsets show them.

# moves/glimmer.toml
[spark_nip]
name = "SPARK NIP"
description = "A QUICK NIP THAT CRACKLES. IT MAY LEAVE THE FOE NUMB."
effect = "damage"
essence = "lightning"
power = 30
accuracy = 100
status = "paralysis"
status_chance = 10
vfx = "strike"

[static_veil]
name = "GLINT VEIL"
description = "SPARKS HANG ROUND THE USER, RAISING ITS DEFENSE."
effect = "stat"
learn_level = 5
stat = "defense"
stat_target = "self"
stat_stages = 1
KeyWhat it is
nameIts name, as a species' (at most 16), never one of the game's own moves' or another of the server's
descriptionPlain letters, at most 120
effectdamage (an attack: needs power), guard (halves damage for the turn, goes first), recover (needs heal), status (needs status) or stat (needs stat)
essenceAn essence, or neutral (left out) for a plain move
power1 to 100 for an attack, 0 for anything else
accuracyPercent, 1 to 100 (default 100)
usesUses before a rest, 1 to 40; left out, the game's rule (by effect and power)
priority0 to 2, an attack only: higher goes first
learn_levelThe least level it is taught at, 1 to 100 (default 1)
status, status_chanceburn, poison, frost, paralysis or sleep, and its chance for an attack (a status move's is always 100)
stat, stat_target, stat_stages, stat_chanceattack, defense, speed or accuracy; self or foe; -2 to 2, not 0; its chance for an attack (a stat move's is 100)
drain, recoilAn attack's percent of its damage healed, or taken back (0 to 100)
explodeAn attack after which its user faints
heal, cureA recovery's percent of health, and whether it clears a condition
vfxHow it looks: projectile, beam, strike, ground_burst, storm or aura; left out, a shot for an attack and an aura for anything else

A server has at most 512 moves of its own. Plugins find them as any other (Move.named by name or key, Creature.teach), and species (Species.named, World.spawn_creature, Team.add). Their numbers are this server's for as long as it runs: keep them by key.

An example pack (the GLIMMERKIT with its two moves and a spawn rule) is in the game's source as plugins-examples/packs/creatures.

Items

Each table in an items/*.toml file is an item of the server's own, keyed pack:table (gems:ruby_sword). Players carry it in their bag, hold it, drop it, store it in chests, wear it and trade for it as any other: their headsets are sent the item with the server's content as they join and its model the first time they see it (in a hand, on the ground, in a bag, on the belt or a holster, in a shop), so nothing is installed. Each item behaves as one of the game's own kinds, by the game's own rules for that kind and its tier:

# items/gems.toml
[ruby]
name = "RUBY"
model = "ruby"
behaviour = "material"
size = 0.18
worth = 12

[ruby_sword]
name = "RUBY SWORD"
model = "ruby_sword"         # marks #grip and #head
behaviour = "sword"
tier = 2
damage = 9
size = 1.0
worth = 140
KeyWhat it is
nameIts name, as a species' (capitals, at most 16), never one of the game's own items' or another of the server's. Make it your own
modelIts model (a file of this pack's models/, or pack:file). A held one (every tool, weapon, bow, rod and shield) marks its #grip; a bow its #string; a tool or weapon its #head (else the half of it past its grip strikes). Its #collision boxes are what lies on the ground and in a hand (else its whole box). A worn piece's model is stretched over its body part (below)
behaviourmaterial (does nothing of itself: crafted from, traded), food (eaten: healing), sword, knife, axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, hammer, club (swung and strike as the game's; pickaxes, axes and shovels dig as theirs of the tier), bow (drawn by its string, shooting the bag's arrows), fishing_rod, shield (raised, blocks), helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots (put on at the pack door's equipment page) or object (placed as an object of its model, by its pack's objects rule)
tier0 (wood) to 5 (abyssal): copper 1, iron 2, cobalt 3, voxelium 4. A tool's blow and digging, a shield's block and armour's protection are the game's for its kind at that tier
damageA tool's or weapon's blow, or a bow's shot, 0 to 100; left out, its tier's (a sword: 4, then 2 more a tier)
healingWhat eating it restores, 0 to 40 (food)
block, protectionA shield's share of a blow it stops, an armour piece's share of harm it takes, percent (0 to 90); left out, its tier's
stackThe most in one slot: a material up to 64 (the default), food and objects up to 16, everything else 1
worthIts worth in coins, what a shop asks where its rule names no price (0, the default: never sold but where a rule names a price)
sizeIts longest side in metres, 0.03 to 3; left out, its model's own (a block is a metre)

Worn armour of a pack protects as the game's does and is drawn on the body, its model stretched over the body part as the game's own armour is fitted, its front (Blockbench's north, -Z, as an object's) the way the wearer faces: a helmet over the head, a chestplate over the torso, leggings one leg's piece from hip to knee drawn on each thigh, boots one foot's with its ankle drawn on each foot, each moving with its bone. Every other player sees it, and the wearer in a mirror; the wearer's own first-person body leaves off the helmet and the chestplate, which would stand at the eyes looking down. A Voxelmon may hold a pack's item as it holds the game's. A server has at most 512 items of its own. Each player's bag, the world's chests, stations and the ground keep them by key: if a pack is taken away, its items stay where they were, as grey boxes nobody can use, and are themselves again when it returns. Plugins find them as any other (ItemType.named by name or key, ItemType.key, ItemType.all), put them in bags (Bag.add) and loot, and loot rules name them by key or name.

Recipes

Each table in a recipes/*.toml file is a recipe made at one of the game's stations, as players make the game's own there: laid out and struck (the workbench, anvil and fletching bench), fed to a forge's mouth, dropped into a cauldron or pot, set on a campfire's rim. Its items are this pack's by their tables, another pack's as pack:item, or the game's own by their names. The station's browser lists it after the game's own.

# recipes/gems.toml
[ruby_sword]
at = "anvil"
output = "ruby_sword"
rows = [" R ", " R ", " S "]
R = "ruby"
S = "STICK"

[ruby_from_crystal]
at = "forge"
output = "ruby"
count = 2
inputs = ["1 RESONANCE CRYSTAL"]
seconds = 12
KeyWhat it is
atworkbench, anvil (a heated one, by a forge), fletching (the fletching bench), forge (its mouth: one input, a log burnt as its fuel), cauldron (with its water, stirred), campfire (its rim: one input) or pot (a cooking pot over a campfire: one to three ingredients, stirred)
output, countWhat it makes and how many, 1 to 64 (default 1; a pot meal's servings, at most 8)
inputsShapeless: each "COUNT ITEM", nine items in all at most
rowsShaped (workbench, anvil, fletching bench): up to three rows of up to three letters, each letter a key of its own in the table naming its item (R = "ruby"), a space for nothing. Laid anywhere on the grid
mirrorA shaped recipe also made mirrored (default false)
secondsHow long the forge smelts it (default 10) or the rim cooks it (default 8)
expThe profession EXP a make pays, 0 to 500: Carpentry at the workbench and fletching bench, Blacksmithing at the anvil and forge, Alchemy at the cauldron, Cooking at the campfire and pot. Left out, what the game's comparable recipes there pay: 6 at the workbench, 24 at the anvil, 16 at the fletching bench, 6 at the forge, 12 at the cauldron, 4 on the rim, 12 in the pot

An anvil's or fletching bench's guide lays a pack's recipe out on its grid as it does the game's own, once a player chooses it in the browser.

A recipe whose items one of the game's own already takes at the same station is never made (the game's come first): the server says so when it reads the pack. A server has at most 512 recipes of its own.

Shops

Each table in a shops/*.toml file adds goods to what a townsperson of one trade sells, with their prices. Their shop page shows a rule's goods first, then the trade's own as room allows (four rows), or the rules' goods alone where one replaces them.

# shops/gems.toml
[blacksmith_rubies]
job = "blacksmith"
town = "any"
sell = ["15 ruby", "160 ruby_sword"]
replace = false
KeyWhat it is
jobThe trade: blacksmith, carpenter, merchant, cook, farmer, innkeeper, baker, librarian, miller, lookout, woodcutter, fisher, herder, orchardist, florist, guard, stargazer, aquarist, petkeeper or miner (the clinic's healer sells nothing)
townany (default), threatened (its Voxelord still there) or freed
sellUp to four, each "PRICE ITEM", a price of 1 to 1,000,000 coins
replacetrue: none of the trade's own goods

Plugins read and set a trade's goods too (World.shop_goods, World.set_shop_goods): a plugin's goods stand for the session in place of a pack's rule for the same trade and towns.

An example pack (a RUBY, a RUBY SWORD authored lying along Z and a RUBY HELMET, the sword and helmet forged on the anvil, rubies smelted from a resonance crystal, and a blacksmith selling the ruby and the sword) is in the game's source as plugins-examples/packs/gems; the creatures pack's GLIMMERKIT drops its rubies.

With plugins

Packs are applied first, then plugins hear of it: a plugin's LootRolled sees a pack's items among the loot, and its CreatureSpawning sees the pack's species, and either may change them again.

Not yet

Sounds and music of a pack's own (no recorded audio travels: a species calls with one of the game's cries); item behaviours of a pack's own beyond the game's kinds (an item does what one of the game's does, never something new: that is a plugin's work); code run on players' headsets; world generation (new blocks, ores, biomes or structures). Each would come with a later game update, and this page will say when.
