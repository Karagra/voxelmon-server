Species

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From Species.named(...), Species.all() or a creature.

A kind of Voxelmon: its essences, its moves, where it lives and what it becomes.

Properties

TypeNameDescription
intidIts lasting number.
StringnameIts name, such as LUNAPUP.
EssenceessenceIts first essence (Fire, Frost, Plant and the others).
Essence?second_essenceIts second essence, if it has one.
RarityrarityHow often it appears in the wild.
TemperamenttemperamentFriendly, neutral or hunting.
List[Habitat]habitatsWhere it lives.
Locomotionmoves_byWalks, swims, both, or flies.
List[Evolution]evolutionsWhat it becomes, and at what level.
Statsbase_statsIts stats before levels.

Methods

ReturnsMethodDescription
Species?named(name: String)A species by name.
List[Species]all()Every species.
List[Move]learns()The moves it learns, and at which levels.

Move

A battle move, found by name (Move.named("Tackle")).

TypeFieldDescription
intidIts lasting number.
StringnameIts name.
Essence?essenceIts essence, or none for a plain move.
intpowerHow hard it hits (0 for a move that does not).
intaccuracyIts chance to land, in percent.

Essence

One of the twelve essences: each strong against two and weak against two.
