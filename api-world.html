World

Planned. The plugin API is being built: nothing here can be used yet, and names may change until the first plugin release.

From server.world().

The world: its blocks and placed objects, loose items, time, weather, towns, dungeons and Voxelmon.

Description

The world is endless and loaded around the players: a block far from everyone is not loaded, and reading it gives nothing (is_loaded). Changing blocks, objects or loose items needs world_edit. Every change reaches players' games as a player's own would.
